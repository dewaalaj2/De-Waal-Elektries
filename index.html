Our Services

Most jobs cross over. A solar install needs the geyser moved; a pressure pump needs both pipe and power. De Waal covers all three, so one team sees the job through.

Electrical
Fault finding & repairs
Tripping breakers, dead plug points and failed lights, traced to the cause.
Rewiring
Old or overloaded wiring replaced, sized and routed for the load.
New builds
Wired from the ground up, including outbuildings and extensions.
Installations
Plug points, lights, fittings and distribution boards, installed and labelled.
Gate motors
Sliding and swing gate motors supplied, installed and repaired.
COC certificates
Certificate of Compliance inspection and sign-off for property transfer and insurance.
Solar & Backup
Panel arrays
Roof-mounted panels sized and angled for Free State sun.
Inverters & batteries
Inverter and battery banks tied into the board, single or dual battery.
Changeover & surge
Changeover switching and surge protection wired into the board.
Plumbing
Geysers
Replacement and repair, plumbed and wired in one visit.
Pipes & leaks
Burst and leaking pipework traced and repaired.
Pumps & tanks
Storage tanks and pressure pumps plumbed in and wired.
